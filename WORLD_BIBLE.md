# World bible

## Master expansion brief mapping — 0.3
Preserve the developed cast: Iri Vale is the Wayfarer; Eda Rook occupies the proposed Mira role; Sella remains Reedwake's researcher and local anchor. Appearance/name/pronoun customization is planned, not implemented. Eda's current surviving signal names the Ninth Relay; a temporal-era memory chronology, distributed navigational presence and reunion are future story development rather than confirmed chapter facts.

Oren's archive intelligence and Veyr's enforced equilibrium are planned additions. Proposed wind/furnace/wetland/observatory allies will be reconciled with the existing Tovan/Neme/Oru/Kes cast without silently discarding established roles. Four order-flexible regional crises, false-return evidence, the Rootsea caretaker and locally controlled-anchor finale remain planned. Their scenes, companions and quests are not in the current build.

## Founding mystery
The Choir of Measures treated weather as infrastructure. Each ceramic engine tuned a different pressure, water or heat pattern. The Sundering occurred nineteen years ago. Official records call it a coordinated failure; new ecosystems flourished around the damage. Bellwether's hidden log instead records an authorized fracture command. Its author and purpose remain unknown.

Today, a renewed pulse at Bellwether tears open a route through the fractured horizon. Expedition researcher Eda Rook disappears with a transit chamber; her tether returns to Reedwake without her. Iri survives with a damaged Loom. Bellwether's emergency buffer carries Eda's voice from after the pulse: she is alive at the Ninth Relay and warns against a network reset. The first chapter establishes that lead; finding the relay and resolving the larger mystery remain planned.

The first pulse event must be an observable scene in the game, with readable action and a return to player control. A caption script alone does not count as a completed opening. “Memory trace” discoveries are brief authored narrative clues; they do not imply the general Echo Lens ability is implemented.

## Cast
- Iri Vale: expedition mechanic; Eda is their expedition partner and trusted friend. Repair is Iri's instinct, but saving someone may require understanding what an unfamiliar machine is protecting.
- Eda Rook: field researcher, missing after the renewed pulse. Curious, dryly funny and willing to test an alarming hypothesis. Her warnings should be specific discoveries, not cryptic instructions designed to delay the plot. She is not the engineer who authorized the fracture nineteen years earlier.
- Sella Venn: Reedwake field researcher and expedition anchor. She cares about people, supplies and the roots she studies. Practical, warm and direct; she helps turn fear for Eda into an achievable next step.
- Tovan Coil: caravan engineer; restored roads would reconnect his scattered family (planned).
- Neme of the Ninth Sail: navigator whose sky home depends on the fracture (planned).
- Elder Oru: Rootsea community leader; surface repairs have flooded ancestral rooms (planned).
- Kes Ardent: rival explorer, useful, reckless and usually one rope short (planned).

## Visual rules
Broad silhouettes before small detail. Cream ceramic bears dark joins and copper edges. Teal light marks Loom-compatible sockets; gold marks discoveries; coral identifies danger together with movement and shape. Plant silhouettes and machine sockets communicate function. Architecture uses open rings, folded textile roofs and suspended counterweights. Landscape composition must frame a compelling destination and maintain depth, atmosphere and readable material differences. Repeated primitive shapes are blockout tools, not a finished visual standard. The rejected v0.1 presentation is being overhauled. No franchise assets or iconography.

## Audio rules
Original synthesized pentatonic fragments, soft wind, hollow ceramic hits and plucked metal. Avoid continuous triumphant scoring. Important machine and enemy sounds have matching visual cues and captions. Asset provenance is maintained in ASSETS.md.
