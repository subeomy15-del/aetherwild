# World bible

## Founding mystery
The Choir of Measures treated weather as infrastructure. Each ceramic engine tuned a different pressure, water or heat pattern. The Sundering occurred nineteen years ago. Engine records call it a coordinated failure; the life that followed suggests a release. Neither interpretation is a complete answer.

## Visual rules
Broad silhouettes before small detail. Cream ceramic bears dark joins and copper edges. Teal light marks Loom-compatible sockets; gold marks discoveries; coral identifies danger together with movement and shape. Plant silhouettes and machine sockets communicate function. Architecture uses open rings, folded textile roofs and suspended counterweights. No franchise assets or iconography.

## Audio rules
Original synthesized pentatonic fragments, soft wind, hollow ceramic hits and plucked metal. Avoid continuous triumphant scoring. Important machine and enemy sounds have matching visual cues and captions. Asset provenance is maintained in ASSETS.md.
